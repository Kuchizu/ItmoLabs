Question 1. Answer: a. Authenticity.
The CIA triad is Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability. Authenticity is not one of them.

Question 2. Answer: a. Confidentiality.
The data is only copied, not changed, and the services keep running. Only confidentiality is broken by the unauthorised disclosure.

Question 3.
Confidentiality: a database breach where an attacker steals customer personal data. The records are disclosed to someone not authorised to see them.
Integrity: an attacker changes an account balance in the bank database. The data is modified without authorisation and no longer correct.
Availability: a DDoS attack floods the server so users cannot reach the website. Authorised users are denied access to the service.

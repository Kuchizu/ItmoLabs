1) 261x ≡ 11 (mod 233)
261 = 233 * 1 + 28
233 = 28 * 8 + 9
28 = 9 * 3 + 1
gcd = 1, one solution.
1 = 28 - 3 * 9 = 25 * 28 - 3 * 233 = 25 * 261 - 28 * 233
261^(-1) ≡ 25 (mod 233)
x ≡ 25 * 11 = 275 ≡ 42 (mod 233)
Answer: x ≡ 42 (mod 233)
2) 119x ≡ 35 (mod 567)
567 = 119 * 4 + 91
119 = 91 * 1 + 28
91 = 28 * 3 + 7
28 = 7 * 4
gcd = 7, 7 | 35, so 7 solutions. Divide by 7: 17x ≡ 5 (mod 81).
From the same steps divided by 7: 1 = 4 * 81 - 19 * 17
17^(-1) ≡ -19 ≡ 62 (mod 81)
x ≡ 62 * 5 = 310 ≡ 67 (mod 81)
Answer: x ∈ {67, 148, 229, 310, 391, 472, 553} (mod 567)
3) 126x ≡ 61 (mod 246)
246 = 126 * 1 + 120
126 = 120 * 1 + 6
120 = 6 * 20
gcd = 6, but 6 does not divide 61.
Answer: no solutions.

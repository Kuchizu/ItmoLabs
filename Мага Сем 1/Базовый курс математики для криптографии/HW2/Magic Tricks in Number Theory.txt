Key fact 65537 = 2^16 + 1 is prime.
Since 2^16 ≡ −1 (mod 65537), squaring gives 2^32 ≡ 1 (mod 65537). So powers of 2 repeat with period 32, and we need the exponent mod 32.
- 2^2 = 4
- 2^4 = 16
- 2^16 = 65536
So the number is 2^65536. Since 65536 = 2^16 = 32 · 2048, we get 65536 ≡ 0 (mod 32), hence
2^65536 = (2^32)^2048 ≡ 1 (mod 65537).
